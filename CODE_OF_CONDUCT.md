# Code of conduct

Everyone is welcome to participate, regardless of background, identity or experience.

Be respectful, give constructive feedback and keep discussions focused on the project.
Disagree with ideas without attacking people. Respect other people's privacy.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, sharing private information
without permission and repeated personal attacks are not acceptable. These rules
apply to project issues, pull requests and other project-managed spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock discussions or restrict participation.
They will consider the context and severity of the behavior when deciding what to do.

To report a problem, contact [@veneito](https://github.com/veneito) using a contact
method listed on their profile. For public incidents, you can also mention the
maintainer in the affected discussion. Do not post private information in a report.
If the report concerns a maintainer, contact another maintainer if one is available;
this small project does not currently have an independent moderation team.
